About Us

About Afro Lodges & Motels

Afro Lodges & Motels is ideally located in Borrowdale, Harare, adjacent to Groombridge Shopping Centre and just minutes from Sam Levy's Village. Guests enjoy easy access to nearby attractions while staying in a quiet, comfortable, and exclusively upmarket setting — perfectly suited for executives, managers, business travellers, and leisure guests seeking premium accommodation in Borrowdale.

We provide luxury lodges and accommodation for individuals, families, and corporate clients. With our professional and friendly staff, every guest is assured of a warm welcome and a memorable stay — truly a home away from home.

In addition to accommodation, Afro Lodges & Motels offers versatile facilities for conferences, business meetings, parties, church gatherings, family events, and guided tours of nearby attractions. Whether visiting for business or leisure, our commitment to comfort and hospitality ensures an unforgettable experience.
